Dining on Taniti

Ten restaurants across three styles of cooking, plus pubs, a microbrewery, and groceries for self-catering.

Local island cooking

Five restaurants serve Taniti's signature fresh fish and rice.

Yellow Leaf Fish House

Harborside dining with the day's catch. Reservations recommended.

Tanitian Table

Family-style local plates near the center of Taniti City.

Bay Reef Grill

Beachfront grill on Yellow Leaf Bay with sunset views.

American-style

Three restaurants serve familiar American meals — an easy option for kids.

Landing Diner

Burgers, breakfasts, and shakes in Merriton Landing.

Island Grill House

Steaks and classic sides, open late.

Pan-Asian

Two restaurants serve Pan-Asian cuisine.

Pacific Wok

Noodles, stir-fry, and sushi near the dance club.

Good to know

Groceries

Two supermarkets, two smaller grocery stores, and one 24-hour convenience store.

Alcohol hours

Alcohol is not served or sold between midnight and 9 a.m. The drinking age is 18.

Holidays

Many restaurants close on Taniti's national holidays — check the holiday info before you plan a night out.
