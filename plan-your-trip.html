Plan Your Trip

The practical answers most visitors ask for, all in one place.

Currency and payments
Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Many businesses also accept euros and yen, and major credit cards are accepted by many businesses. Several banks offer currency exchange.
Power outlets
Outlets are 120 volts — the same as in the United States, so U.S. visitors do not need an adapter.
National holidays and closures
Taniti observes a large number of national holidays, and many attractions and restaurants close on those days. Check holiday dates before booking activities or planning a night out.
Health services
The island has one hospital and several clinics. The hospital has many multilingual employees.
Safety
Violent crime is very rare. As tourism grows there are more reports of pickpocketing and other petty crime, so keep an eye on valuables in busy areas.
Language
Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. Less English is spoken in rural areas, especially among older residents.
Alcohol rules
Alcohol is not served or sold between midnight and 9 a.m. The drinking age is 18.
