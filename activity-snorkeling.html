Snorkeling at Yellow Leaf Bay

Family-friendly
Yellow Leaf Bay — calm, clear water

About this activity

Spend two hours exploring the reef just off the white, sandy beaches that encircle Yellow Leaf Bay. A local guide leads every group, and the sheltered bay stays calm most of the year, which makes this a comfortable first snorkel for kids and adults alike. All gear is included, and a short safety briefing starts every session. Lockers and changing rooms are available at the beach hut.

Quick facts

  • Price: $35 per adult, $20 per child
  • Hours: 9 a.m. – 4 p.m. daily
  • Location: Yellow Leaf Bay beach hut
  • Ages: 6 and up
  • Gear: Mask, snorkel, fins included
