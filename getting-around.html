Getting Around

You don't need a car on Taniti — the city is walkable and well served by buses and taxis.

Island map — airport, Taniti City, Merriton Landing

Walking

Taniti City is fairly flat and very walkable, and the Merriton Landing area is easy to explore on foot.

Public buses

Serve Taniti City every day from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. Private buses serve the rest of the island.

Taxis

Available throughout Taniti City.

Rental cars

Available from a local agency near the airport.

Bikes

Rent bikes and helmets from several vendors. Helmets are required by law.

Arriving

Most visitors arrive by air at Taniti's small airport. A small cruise ship also docks in Yellow Leaf Bay one night per week.
